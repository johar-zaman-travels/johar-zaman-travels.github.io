A few things to know

Frequently asked questions

The essentials to consider before you start your UAE tourist visa enquiry.

Which tourist visa duration should I choose?

The 30 and 60 day single entry tourist visa options offer different lengths of stay. Start with your planned itinerary, then confirm your eligibility and the appropriate option before applying. See GDRFA guidance ↗

What is the total cost?

Request a written quotation for your circumstances. Confirm the government charges, service fees, insurance, applicable taxes and any additional charges before making a payment.

How long will the application take?

Confirm the current processing estimate when your documents are reviewed. Allow time for additional checks and requests from the immigration authority.

Does every traveller need to apply in advance?

Entry requirements depend on nationality and individual circumstances. Check whether you qualify for visa-free entry or a visa on arrival before arranging a tourist visa. Check official entry guidance ↗

How do I send my enquiry?

Choose Submit by email to open a draft in your email app, or Send on WhatsApp to open a chat with your message ready. Review the message and tap Send in the app.

Can a travel agent guarantee approval?

The immigration authority decides whether to issue a visa. Confirm the terms, refund policy and application conditions with the service provider before paying.
